## What M7 set out to do, and what it did

The owner asked for four things on 2026-09-16. Measured, on the oracle server's RTX 4090 unless
said otherwise:

1. **Training easier to use.** One document runs a training (`es train --recipe`, T1) and one
   document runs the whole loop (`es loop cycle`, T2): collect 200 demonstrations → the expert
   through the harness → train → evaluate → showcase in **30:19**, of which evaluation is 20:24
   because a bad policy runs every episode to its budget. §19.3's twelve slots are real values or
   `unset`, never invented (T1, T4, T5, T6).
2. **The editor friendlier.** A run opens as a table, a Safety Plane timeline and a filmstrip
   (E1); a trajectory replays in 3D with no GPU (E2); every parameter has a widget, nodes are
   searchable, files open by drag or from a recent list (E3); a running evaluation publishes on
   four streams and the editor draws it with the same code as a finished one at **+0.16 %** cost
   (E4); the editor starts and kills the run and attaches itself (E5); and, after the owner's
   note of 2026-09-21, the editor speaks in plain words in English or Korean with a font that
   renders Hangul (E6).
3. **Training faster and the task more accurate.** Batched lowering took 20,000 steps from
   675 s to **137 s** (T3, and it found that the token-less transformer had been fed the batch as
   the sequence); warmup + cosine at batch 64 reaches a loss **36 % below** batch 8's at equal
   samples in 26 s against 107 s (T4); the ImageNet backbone enters the chain as a pinned,
   licensed artifact (T5); the Observation IR's `training_only` chain is applied by the trainer
   from one seed (T6); the evaluator runs the collector's latency model (T7); `Env` seeks an
   episode bitwise (T8). The re-measurement (U): on the committed documents under T7's model,
   **U3 (pretrained + augmented) scores held-out 0.5625 and passes the demo's acceptance — the
   first IR-graph policy to do so**; U0 0.25, U2 0.1875, U1 diverged to NaN at step 4,517.
4. **RS and PT quality.** A software two-level BVH took a 1280×720 frame from **80 to 5.2 ms**
   with every output bit unchanged (R1, R1b); shadows, hemisphere ambient, Blinn-Phong and SSAA
   behind `Shading::Full` at 9.9 ms (R2); next-event estimation with MIS, a ReSTIR whose bias
   went from −1.76 % to +0.10 %, a tone map that makes PT emit `Rgb8` bitwise on both sides, and
   the first SSIM numbers for §15.3 (R3); temporal accumulation where `N` frames of 1 spp are
   bitwise one frame of `N` spp, with the variance term SVGF had been missing (R4). The showcase
   now renders in three looks: RS full 9.9, PT 64 spp 262, PT 4 spp accumulated 29.8 ms/frame.

## Findings

### Blocker

None. Every packet's oracles ran (`RAN` lines in the notes), the four numbers of U are on
committed documents with their hashes, and no invariant moved.

### Closed during M7 (each a rule candidate for the spec)

- **F-11 (T3).** The lowered transformer's `unsqueeze(0)` fed the batch as the sequence, so
  `--batch 8` was eight single-sample forwards with wrong attention over them. Fixed by the batch
  axis; `contract.json` now requires `batch_axis` and old module directories must be re-lowered.
- **F-12 (T7).** The evaluator executed chunk row 0 at tick 0 while the collector honoured the
  declared latency, so the two trajectories of one seed diverged from tick 1. Fixed: one latency
  model, parity to the last tick; the IR-graph policy's 0.625 (V18b) re-measures at 0.0625.
- **F-13 (R2, amended at review).** `albedo · (hemi + diffuse)` clipped; now
  `hemi + diffuse · (1 − hemi)`. The Cornell golden could not see it (a closed room); a
  shadows-off pass and an energy-conservation test can.
- **F-14 (R3).** ReSTIR's `1/M` combination was −1.76 % biased against a 4,096 spp reference;
  pairwise MIS with visibility at the final shade brings it to +0.10 %.
- **F-15 (E3, sent back at review).** A plain click did not select a node (selection only on
  drag), so the inspector was unreachable by mouse.
- **F-16 (E4).** The attach source read 4 kB per repaint and the producer dropped 174 of 226
  frames; draining up to 64 chunks per poll drops none.
- **F-17 (T5, then fixed on `main`).** `cargo test -- --include-ignored` ran a golden generator
  and rewrote a golden in a server tree. All nine generators now no-op without
  `ES_GENERATE_GOLDENS=1` (`9aa76f5`).

### Should-fix

- **S-1 (T8).** `SafetyCounters::window` (the `ViolationRate` ring) carries across
  `begin_episode`, so tick 0 of episode `k` is judged on the tail of episode `k−1`. This and
  **S-2** — `StepEvent::tick` is the cell's cumulative physics clock — are the two mechanisms that
  make a `(cell, episode)` partition non-identical to the sequential run (first difference
  `nominal-01` tick 24). The partition was built, measured at **2.0× / 2.2×** (jobs 4 / 8) on
  the 16-episode nominal suite, and not shipped. Both fixes move committed numbers (human decision).
- **S-3 (T6).** `es_ir::observation::image_out` keeps the incoming `ImageSpec` on an `Augment`
  port, so `Pad(4) → Augment{RandomCrop 96×96}` is refused by `TYPE-020`; the fixture spells the
  random shift as `Crop{Random}` instead. One `es-ir` change makes the two spellings equal.
- **S-4 (T2).** `es eval run --expert` was added beyond the packet's scope (the expert gate needs
  it) and infers episode boundaries from an exactly-zero velocity because `es_eval::Evaluation`
  has no episode hook. The hook is the fix; the heuristic fails loudly, not quietly.
- **S-5 (U, open question 36).** `es eval compare` crosses a moved `evaluation_hash` silently;
  `es loop cycle` refuses it. One rule.
- **S-6 (E5).** `kill()` terminates the parent only; `--jobs N` workers and the trainer survive.
  A job object / process group.
- **S-7 (E4, E5).** A launched or attached live run is invisible while a finished run directory
  is open (`run_table` matches the directory first). One shared clearing rule.
- **S-8 (E4).** `CLIENT_QUEUE_CAPACITY = 16` is a depth limit, not §23.3's per-step budget; with
  `--telemetry-image-every 1` a viewer loses most images by design. A per-stream rate limiter.
- **S-9 (U, open question 35).** The pretrained arm has no learning rate shown to converge
  without augmentation: U1 diverged at 4e-4, U3 (with augmentation) did not. `grad_clip` exists
  (T4) and has never been used in a measured run.
- **S-10 (pre-existing, B1b S-12).** `quadruped_eval_run_names_the_observation_gap` fails on a
  machine that has both mujoco and torch: the bundle's placeholder weights are refused before the
  observation-gap refusal the test pins. Green in CI only because CI has no torch.
- **S-11 (M5 S-4, still).** `transport::tests::a_connection_past_max_clients_is_refused` flakes
  under CPU load.
- **S-12 (R3, R4).** The showcase's default `--exposure 1` renders both committed scenes at a
  mean byte of 23–33 against the rasterizer's 130–173; every PT video in M7 used 32. §15.3's SSIM
  threshold is still unset (0.29–0.32 at exposure 1, ~0.59/0.65 exposure-matched).
- **S-13 (U).** There is no CLI that packs a bundle from four documents; U1/U3's bundles were
  built by running a test in a scratch tree. `es policy pack --documents <dir>`.
- **S-14 (T8, inherent).** Under the `(cell, episode)` partition each worker opens its own
  MuJoCo and torch subprocess; the 2× ceiling is that, not the physics.

### Nit

- The E3 status line's "N edits" is `history().len()`, so it stays 1 after Undo.
- `es train --dry-run` on the IR route prints the plan without T6's `--for-training` and
  `--augmentation` flags (it opens no bundle); the executed `config.json` carries them.
- `eframe`'s `persistence` feature (pulls `ron`) was accepted for E3's recent list.
- R2's 1-of-4,096 edge-tie CPU/GPU tolerance: pin `dot` in `es_intersect` in its own packet.
- R4's primitive id is the triangle index, so a quad's diagonal crossing a pixel drops history.
- R3/R4's accumulation and ReSTIR are not combined (ReSTIR overwrites the accumulated buffer).

### Human decision

- **The stop rule (§28.10).** Literal: U3's 0.5625 does not exceed V18b's 0.625, IR-graph tuning
  ends, the product is the external route's speed. Like-for-like: V18b's 0.625 was zero-latency
  and re-measures at 0.0625 under T7, so U3 is the first IR-graph policy to pass the demo's
  acceptance on the evaluator the project now runs, at 9× the honest baseline. **Recommendation:
  re-state the bar as "passes the demo's acceptance under the declared latency" (which U3 does),
  and allow exactly one more T-packet — a learning-rate/`grad_clip` sweep for the pretrained arm
  (S-9) — before the rule is applied again.** The owner chooses.
- **Episode-boundary semantics (S-1, S-2).** Clearing the `ViolationRate` window at
  `begin_episode` and making `StepEvent::tick` episode-relative is INV-12-clean (the envelope,
  watchdogs and counters stay on) and arguably §13.1's meaning, and it changes every committed
  demo number. Yes buys the 2× partition (T8b, small); no keeps the record as is.
- **§15.3's SSIM threshold** (S-12): set it from the exposure-matched figure (~0.6), tune PT's
  lights to the RS model, or restate §15.3 as "geometry bitwise, RGB is the lighting model's".
- **R5/R6 (mesh geoms, MJCF textures)** move `scene_hash` and invalidate every committed
  checkpoint; still parked.
- **`Cargo.lock` (M5 R7)** is still untracked; `hardware.json` therefore names a build that is
  not reproducible from the repository. **The `lerobot` pin** at 0.6.1 (§28.10 decision 3).
- **M6 quadruped**: still parked.
- **Push.** `origin/main` is at `28bdae4`; local `main` is 69 commits ahead at this review.

## Verdict

**M7: passed.** Each of the four goals has a number on a committed document, and the three rules
the plan fixed on day one held: not one observation pixel moved, `es train` invented no digest,
and the editor decided nothing in `app.rs`. The result that matters for the spec is the U table
read the honest way: with the batch axis, the schedule, the ImageNet backbone and the
document's own augmentation, an IR-graph policy passes the demo's acceptance on the evaluator
that also runs the collector's latency — and the one arm that failed (U1) failed by diverging,
not by learning nothing, which is a learning-rate question and not an architecture one. What
M7 cost is in S-1..S-14: the two episode-boundary semantics the evaluator inherited from M5
(window, absolute tick) are the only thing between the project and a 2× evaluation, and both are
the owner's call because both move the record. The safety invariants held throughout without a
single line of `es-safety` changing.
